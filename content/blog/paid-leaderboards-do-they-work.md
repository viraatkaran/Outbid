Pay-to-rank leaderboards are cheap, visible, and easy to try. They are also easy to over-credit.

## What they can do
- Give a new product a few hundred qualified visits.
- Provide a backlink and a social proof badge.
- Create a fun moment to share ("we just hit #1").

## What they cannot do
- Replace a product that people want.
- Guarantee sales. Ask for verified clicks, not vanity numbers.

## How to measure
Add UTM parameters, compare signups to clicks, and divide spend by signups. If a placement costs $50 and brings 5 signups, your cost per signup is $10. Decide whether that beats your other channels.
