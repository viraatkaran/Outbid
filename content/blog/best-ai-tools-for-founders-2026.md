There are thousands of AI tools and most founders only need a handful. Here is a short, practical way to choose.

## 1. Start from the job, not the tool
Write down the three tasks that cost your team the most hours each week (support replies, content drafts, lead research). Search for tools by task.

## 2. Check for proof, not promises
Look for: real customer logos, a public changelog, transparent pricing, and a free trial you can finish in one afternoon.

## 3. Compare two or three, not twenty
Run the same real task through each candidate and score the output. Keep a simple sheet: quality, speed, cost, and how much editing was needed.

## 4. Mind the lock-in
Can you export your data? Is there an API? A cheap tool you cannot leave is expensive later.
