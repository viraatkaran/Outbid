No audience? That is normal. Here is a launch plan that works with zero followers.

## Before launch
- Write one sentence: "We help X do Y without Z."
- Build a simple landing page with an email signup.
- Collect 20 conversations with people who have the problem.

## Launch week
1. Post a founder story (what you built, why, what is still broken).
2. Share in the communities where your users already hang out; follow each community's rules.
3. List your product on directories and leaderboards, including ours, and track which sources send people who sign up.

## After launch
Reply to every message, ship visible fixes weekly, and write down what you learned. Measure clicks, signups, and cost per signup so you know which channel deserves more money.
